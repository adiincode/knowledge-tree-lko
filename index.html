WHY FAMILIES CHOOSE US

Built like a tree — strong root, honest growth.

Knowledge Tree began as a single Lucknow classroom and grew the way a tree does: one steady root, then branches added only when the last one was strong. That's still how we open new centres today.

One curriculum, four locations

Lucknow, Sitapur, Kanpur and our online classroom all follow the same syllabus and testing schedule, so a student can move between them without losing pace.

25 teachers, small batches

Every subject is handled by a teacher who specialises in it, and batch sizes are kept small enough for doubts to actually get answered in class.

Olympiad training built in

Alongside school and board preparation, we run dedicated batches for national and international Olympiad exams across Maths, Science and English.
